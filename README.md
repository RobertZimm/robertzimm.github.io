# robertzimm.github.io

Personal website of **Robert Zimmermann**, PhD researcher at the Institute of Information Processing (TNT), Leibniz University Hannover.

My research focuses on the interpretability of visual foundation models — adapting models like DINOv2 so their decisions can be understood by humans.

The site itself is published at [robertzimm.github.io](https://robertzimm.github.io) and hosts my publications, CV, projects, and teaching.

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) on top of the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) theme.
